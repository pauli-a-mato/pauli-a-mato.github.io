The logistics

While weddings may seem like a big social obligation, ours is not. We will be happy for everyone who accepts the invitation, but if you can't attend or simply don't feel like socializing with 100 other people, we respect that.

We're sorry you can't make it, but we completely understand. If you’d like to see us outside our wedding day, we'd love a visit or a trip together. If you find out after sending the RSVP that you cannot attend, please let us or our close ones know by phone or message (this helps us with planning). Thank you.

In the "Additional Information" section, we provide a list of accommodations near the wedding venue. If you need transport to nearby villages or Žilina on the wedding day, we will arrange it.

In the "Additional Information" section, you’ll find tips on how to get to the wedding venue. If you’d like to travel with other guests, please use the provided contact details and ask our close friends who can help connect you with other guests.

Each of our guests has been given the contact information of one of our dearest friends, who will try to answer any further questions!

We are working on a wedding playlist, and we will try to incorporate your suggestions from the RSVP form. Of course, you can also request a song at the venue, but please respect our DJ and the musical atmosphere. :)
